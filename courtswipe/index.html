Scoring on Apple Watch

How do I score a point?

Swipe anywhere on the watch screen. Swipe right to left to score for You, and left to right for your opponent. Your score is always on the left in lime and your opponent's on the right in blue, matching the swipe directions. Each point plays a tap on your wrist.

I scored the wrong point. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo in the bottom bar. Undo takes back the last point, set or server change, one step at a time. In automatic formats you can even undo the match point from the summary screen with Undo Last Point.

How do I change who serves?

At the very start of a set (0–0), tap the score of the player who should serve. The * next to a name in the header marks the server. After that, the serve switches automatically every game, and every two points in a tiebreak.

Where did the top and bottom bars go?

That's Zoom Score: during a game the bars hide so the score fills the screen. They come back between games, or when you tap the score. You can turn it off in Settings (the gear in the bottom bar).

What's the difference between Quick Match and a planned match?

Quick Match uses Manual scoring: you decide when each set ends. When a set is won, the bottom bar shows Next Set and End, plus TB7 at 6–6 and TB10 when the sets are level. Swipe the bar to switch between these buttons and Undo.

Planned matches (added on your iPhone) use the format you chose: the watch closes sets, starts tiebreaks and ends the match automatically.

What does "Deciding Point" mean?

In No-Ad scoring there's no advantage: at 40–40 the next point wins the game. The watch shows "DECIDING POINT" when that point comes up. Turn on No-Ad in the match's format on iPhone, or in watch Settings before the first point.

Formats

Which formats are supported?

Best of 1, 3 or 5 sets; 4, 6 or 8 games per set; a tiebreak to 7 at 6–6 (or 4–4 / 8–8 for short or long sets); an optional 10-point match tiebreak in place of the deciding set; No-Ad; and fixed-game sets (for example exactly 4 games per set, with the winner decided by total games). Quick Match is fully Manual.

Can I change a match's format after it's played?

No. The format can be edited while the match is upcoming, and is locked once it has a result, so your stats stay consistent.

iPhone and syncing

My planned match doesn't appear on the watch.

Check that:

  • The match is scheduled between 12 hours ago and 14 days from now (up to 20 matches are sent).
  • Your Apple Watch is paired with this iPhone and the CourtSwipe watch app is installed (Watch app on iPhone → My Watch → scroll to Available Apps).
  • Open the iPhone app once after adding the match; it sends the list when it changes.

On the watch, start a new match (Settings → New Match) to see the updated list.

A result from the watch hasn't shown up on my iPhone.

Open the iPhone app. Results arrive immediately when the iPhone app is running, and otherwise they're queued by the system and delivered the next time it opens. Keep Bluetooth on and your iPhone near the watch. Results are also stored on the watch, so nothing is lost while you wait.

Can I use the iPhone app without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Plan matches and enter results by hand with Record Result in any saved match. Swipe scoring and point-by-point stats need the watch app.

Why don't some matches have Point Stats?

Point stats come from the point log the watch records while you score. Results entered by hand on iPhone only have set scores.

I reopened a finished match with Undo. What happens on my iPhone?

The result is withdrawn from your iPhone until you finish the match again. A planned match goes back to your schedule, and a Quick Match result is removed.

Privacy and data

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. There's no account and no server: your matches stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. See the Privacy Policy below.

How do I delete my matches?

Swipe to delete in Schedule, or open a match and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 27 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 27 or later for scoring.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

The short version
  • CourtSwipe does not collect, store or share any personal data on any server.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your matches stay on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

Who we are

CourtSwipe (the "app") is an iPhone app with a companion Apple Watch app, made by Andy Fuentes, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to information you enter in the app.

Information the app stores

To work, the app keeps the following information only on your own devices:

  • Matches you plan: the opponent name you type, the date and time, and the scoring format.
  • Match results: the games won in each set and, for matches scored on Apple Watch, a log of each point (who won it, who served, and when it was played).
  • Settings: display preferences, such as Zoom Score on the watch.

This information never leaves your devices except as described in the next section. I can't see it, and I have no server that receives it.

Sharing between your iPhone and Apple Watch

The app sends your upcoming matches from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch, and finished match results from the watch back to your iPhone. This uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, which transfers data directly between your own devices. It doesn't go through any server operated by me.

What the app does not do

  • It doesn't require an account or sign-in.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet or to any server operated by me or anyone else.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code (SDKs).
  • It doesn't track you across apps or websites.
  • It doesn't access your location, health data, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It doesn't sell or share any information with anyone.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup, like any other app's. Apple handles those backups under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete any match in the app (swipe to delete in Schedule, or Delete in the match editor). Deleting the app from your iPhone or Apple Watch removes all of its data from that device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
