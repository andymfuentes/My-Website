Frequently asked questions

How do I score a point?

Swipe anywhere on the Play screen. Right-to-left scores a point for you; left-to-right scores a point for your opponent.

I swiped by accident — how do I fix the score?

Tap the bar at the bottom of the Play screen once to undo the last point. Keep tapping to undo further, all the way back to the start of the match.

How do I restart a match?

Press and hold the bottom bar. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is discarded.

Can I change the match format (best of 3 vs. 5)?

Yes. Tap Match Settings on the watch's Start screen and pick a preset: Friday Night Social, Ladder, Play Session, BestOf3 Match (the default) or BestOf5 Match, plus any you've created. Your choice applies immediately, but since Start only shows between matches, it never affects one already in progress.

Can I create my own format, or change a built-in one?

Yes, in the iPhone companion app: tap the sliders icon to add a preset or edit one (match length, singles or doubles, games per set, rotating partners, No-Ad, 10-point deciding set). Edited built-ins can be reset to the original. Changes sync to your watch automatically. The watch itself only selects presets — the bigger screen makes editing much easier.

Does CourtSwipe support doubles?

Yes. Pick a doubles preset (such as Friday Night Social or Play Session) and the scoreboard shows the teams — You/P2 against P3/P4. You still swipe for your side or the other side. In a 3-set doubles match you can rotate partners each set (You/P2, then You/P3, then You/P4); the results then show every player's games individually and name the top two.

Can a match end in a tie?

Yes, in the fixed-length formats (1 set or 3 sets of a set number of games), where every game is played. If the score finishes level, the result is a tie. Normal best-of-3 and best-of-5 matches always have a winner.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes, there's an optional companion app. It mirrors your live score from the watch, keeps an archive of finished matches, and is where you create and edit match-format presets. Scoring itself always happens on your Apple Watch, and the watch works with no phone at all — including all five built-in presets.

Does CourtSwipe need an internet connection?

No. CourtSwipe works entirely offline. It doesn't connect to the internet for anything.

Where is my match history stored? Does it sync anywhere?

Your match history is stored locally on your watch. If you use the iPhone companion app, finished matches are also copied to your paired iPhone over the direct watch-to-phone connection (your Match Settings presets travel the same way). Nothing is backed up to iCloud or any other service, or shared anywhere — see the Privacy Policy below.

I lost my match history after deleting/reinstalling — why?

Match history is stored only on your devices. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device, the same as any other app's local data. There's currently no cloud backup. Matches deleted on one device aren't deleted on the other, so the iPhone archive can still have matches you removed from the watch.

How many matches does Match History keep?

Your most recent 50 matches on the watch (100 on the iPhone companion). Older matches are automatically removed as new ones are added.

Does CourtSwipe cost anything beyond the purchase price?

No. CourtSwipe is a one-time $1.99 purchase with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE — set before publishing]

CourtSwipe ("the app") is developed by Alejandro. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Summary

CourtSwipe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your Apple Watch and, if you use the optional iPhone companion app, your own paired iPhone.

What CourtSwipe stores, and where

CourtSwipe saves, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults):

  • Match history — your finished tennis matches: winner, final score, duration, and basic stats like break count and serve percentage, plus the opponent name you entered, if any
  • Opponent names you've typed, so you can pick them again (on the watch only)
  • Match Settings presets — the match formats you've created or edited (for example "best of 3, No-Ad"). These contain no personal information

This data:

  • Never leaves your own devices
  • Is never transmitted over the internet — CourtSwipe makes no network connections of any kind. The only transfer is Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity link between your Apple Watch and your paired iPhone, which sends your live score and finished matches to the iPhone companion app, and keeps your Match Settings presets the same on both devices
  • Is not synced to iCloud or any other service
  • Is not shared with the developer or any third party
  • Is automatically limited (your most recent 50 matches on the watch and 100 on the iPhone; 20 opponent names; 20 saved presets)
  • Is deleted if you delete the app (on the device you delete it from)

What CourtSwipe does NOT do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs or dependencies of any kind
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or any other sensitive device capability — the app never requests any of these permissions
  • No cookies or web tracking (the app doesn't use a web view or make web requests)

Children's privacy

Because CourtSwipe collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and there is nothing to collect regardless of a user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds cloud sync), this document will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly. As of this version, the statement above is complete and exhaustive — there is no hidden data collection beyond what's described here.
