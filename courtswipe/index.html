Frequently asked questions

How do I score a point?

Swipe anywhere on the Play screen. Right-to-left scores a point for you; left-to-right scores a point for your opponent.

I swiped by accident — how do I fix the score?

Tap the bar at the bottom of the Play screen once to undo the last point. Keep tapping to undo further, all the way back to the start of the match.

How do I restart a match?

Press and hold the bottom bar. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is discarded.

Can I change the match format (best of 3 vs. 5)?

Yes — match settings live right on the Start screen, no separate Settings screen needed. Changes apply immediately, but since Start only shows between matches, they never affect one already in progress.

Does CourtSwipe support doubles?

Not currently — CourtSwipe is built for singles matches only.

Is there an iPhone app?

No. CourtSwipe is a watch-only app — everything runs directly on your Apple Watch, with no phone required at all.

Does CourtSwipe need an internet connection?

No. CourtSwipe works entirely offline and never connects to the internet for anything.

Where is my match history stored? Does it sync anywhere?

Your match history is stored locally on your watch only. It isn't backed up to iCloud or any other service, and isn't shared anywhere — see the Privacy Policy below.

I lost my match history after deleting/reinstalling — why?

Match history is stored only on-device. Deleting the app removes it, the same as any other app's local data. There's currently no cloud backup.

How many matches does Match History keep?

Your most recent 50 matches. Older matches are automatically removed as new ones are added.

Does CourtSwipe cost anything beyond the purchase price?

No. CourtSwipe is a one-time $1.99 purchase with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE — set before publishing]

CourtSwipe ("the app") is developed by [Alejandro]. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Summary

CourtSwipe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your Apple Watch.

What CourtSwipe stores, and where

CourtSwipe saves a local history of your finished tennis matches — winner, final score, duration, and basic stats like break count and serve percentage — directly on your Apple Watch, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted over the internet — CourtSwipe makes no network connections of any kind
  • Is not synced to iCloud or any other service
  • Is not shared with the developer or any third party
  • Is automatically limited to your most recent 50 matches
  • Is deleted if you delete the app

What CourtSwipe does NOT do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs or dependencies of any kind
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or any other sensitive device capability — the app never requests any of these permissions
  • No cookies or web tracking (the app doesn't use a web view or make web requests)

Children's privacy

Because CourtSwipe collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and there is nothing to collect regardless of a user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional iPhone sync), this document will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly. As of this version, the statement above is complete and exhaustive — there is no hidden data collection beyond what's described here.
