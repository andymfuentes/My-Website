Frequently asked questions

How do I score a point?

Swipe anywhere on the Play screen. Right to left scores a point for you; left to right scores a point for your opponent. A haptic tap confirms each one.

My swipes aren't scoring. Why?

Swipes are ignored while your wrist is down (the screen is dimmed) and while the workout is paused (the screen shows PAUSED). Raise your wrist, or swipe up and tap Resume.

I swiped by mistake. How do I fix the score?

Tap Undo (bottom left of the Play screen) to take back the last point. Keep tapping to go back further, all the way to the start of the match.

How do I end a match early?

Tap Exit (the door, bottom right of the Play screen) and confirm Exit Game. Games played so far are saved to Match History. With a workout running, you can also swipe up and tap End Match. Either way, the workout keeps running.

Can I change the match format (best of 3 or 5, No-Ad, doubles)?

Yes. On the Start screen, tap the NEXT MATCH card, then Match Settings, and pick one of the seven formats: Friday Night Social, Ladder, Play Session, BestOf3 Match (the default), BestOf3 No-Ads, BestOf5 Match or BestOf5 No-Ads. Formats can't be edited or created yet.

Why did my format and opponent reset?

The format, opponent and first server you pick last until you close the app. When you reopen CourtSwipe, it starts again at BestOf3 Match against "Opponent". Recent opponent names stay saved, so you can pick one again quickly.

Does CourtSwipe support doubles?

Yes. Pick Friday Night Social or Play Session and the scoreboard shows the teams, You/P2 against P3/P4. You still swipe for your side or the other side. Friday Night Social rotates your partner each set (P2, then P3, then P4), and the summary shows each player's games and names the top two.

Can a match end in a tie?

Yes, in the fixed-length formats (Friday Night Social and Play Session), where every game is played. Best of 3 and best of 5 always have a winner.

Are my workouts saved to Apple Health?

Yes. When a workout ends (End Workout, auto-stop, or turning workouts off), it is saved to Health as a Tennis workout with its duration, heart rate and active calories. It appears in the Health and Fitness apps and counts toward your Activity rings. Ending a match doesn't end the workout, because one workout can cover several matches.

It says "Couldn't start a workout." What do I do?

CourtSwipe needs Health access to run a workout. On your watch, open Settings → Health → Apps → CourtSwipe, or on iPhone open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps → CourtSwipe. Turn the permissions on. Scoring works without it.

Can I score without a workout?

Yes. Turn Workout off in Settings. The Play screen then shows only the score, with no heart rate or calories.

What is auto-stop?

In Settings you can have the workout end itself after 1 or 2 hours of active time (paused time doesn't count), in case you forget to end it. The workout is saved, and scoring carries on.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. CourtSwipe runs entirely on Apple Watch. An iPhone companion is planned for a later version.

Does CourtSwipe need an internet connection?

No. CourtSwipe works entirely offline.

Where is my match history stored?

On your Apple Watch only. It isn't backed up to iCloud or shared anywhere. See the Privacy Policy below.

How many matches does Match History keep?

Your 50 most recent. Older matches are removed automatically as new ones are added.

I lost my match history after deleting the app. Why?

Match history is stored only on the watch, so deleting the app removes it. Workouts saved to Apple Health are not affected.

Does CourtSwipe cost anything beyond the purchase price?

No. CourtSwipe is a one-time $1.99 purchase with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE — set before publishing]

CourtSwipe ("the app") is developed by Alejandro. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it's kept, and what it never does.

Summary

CourtSwipe does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The developer never receives any of your data. Your match history stays on your Apple Watch. Workout data you allow CourtSwipe to use stays in Apple Health, which you control.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

CourtSwipe can run a tennis workout while you play. With your permission it:

  • Reads your heart rate and active energy (calories) during a workout, to show them live and on the end-of-match summary.
  • Saves each finished tennis workout to Apple Health, with its duration, heart rate and active calories, so it appears in the Health and Fitness apps and counts toward your Activity rings.

You choose these permissions in the Health permission sheet, and you can change them at any time: on Apple Watch in Settings → Health → Apps → CourtSwipe, or on iPhone in the Health app (your profile → Apps → CourtSwipe). If you don't grant access, scoring still works without workout features.

Health data:

  • Is used only to provide these workout features on your device
  • Is never sent to the developer, never transmitted over the internet, and never shared with third parties
  • Is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining
  • Is not copied into CourtSwipe's own storage: match history does not include heart rate or calories

Workouts saved to Apple Health are managed by Apple's Health app. Deleting CourtSwipe does not delete them; you can delete them in the Health app.

What CourtSwipe stores on your watch

Using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults), CourtSwipe keeps:

  • Match history: your recent matches, with the winner, set scores, match duration, serve and break statistics, and the opponent name you entered, if any
  • Opponent names you've typed, so you can pick them again
  • Settings: whether workouts are on, and the workout auto-stop time

This data:

  • Stays on your Apple Watch and is never transmitted. CourtSwipe makes no network connections of any kind
  • Is not synced to iCloud or any other service
  • Is not shared with the developer or any third party
  • Is limited automatically: your most recent 50 matches and 20 opponent names
  • Is deleted when you delete the app

What CourtSwipe does NOT do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting services or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs or dependencies
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera
  • No cookies or web tracking (the app has no web views and makes no web requests)

Children's privacy

CourtSwipe collects no data from anyone, so it does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds an iPhone app or cloud sync), this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. This policy describes everything the app does with data. There is no other data collection.
