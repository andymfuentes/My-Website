Made for the court

Eyes on the ball, not on tiny buttons.

Swipe to score

Swipe right to left for you, left to right for your opponent, anywhere on the screen. A tap on your wrist confirms each point.

🎾

Real tennis rules

Deuce and advantage, No-Ad, tiebreaks to 7, match tiebreaks to 10, and automatic server tracking.

↩️

Undo anything

Mis-swiped? Undo takes back the last point, set or server change. In automatic formats it even reopens a match after match point.

🗓️

Plan on iPhone

Add matches with the opponent, time and format. They're waiting on your watch, ready to start with one tap.

📊

Stats for every point

Each result comes back to your iPhone with the full point log, turned into serve, return and pressure stats.

🔎

Zoom Score

During a game the bars step aside and the score fills the screen. Big, bold, readable at a glance.

How it works

From the schedule to the stats in three steps.

Plan

Add a match on iPhone: best of 3, 6 games, No-Ad, whatever your league plays.

Play

Pick it on your watch and swipe after every point. Sets and tiebreaks take care of themselves.

Review

The result lands on your iPhone with your record, streak and point-by-point stats.
