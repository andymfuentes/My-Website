## Full description

```
CourtSwipe is a tennis scorekeeper built entirely for your Apple Watch.
No phone to dig out between points — just swipe.

SWIPE TO SCORE
Swipe right-to-left when you win a point, left-to-right when you don't.
A quick haptic tap confirms every point, so you know it registered
without even looking down.

FULLY AUTOMATIC SCORING
CourtSwipe handles the real rules of tennis so you don't have to think
about them mid-match:
 • Points, games, and sets
 • Deuce and advantage
 • Tiebreaks at 6-6
 • Optional no-ad scoring
 • Optional 10-point final-set tiebreak
 • Serve tracking that alternates correctly, even inside a tiebreak

UNDO ANYTHING
Mis-swiped? Tap once to undo the last point — keep tapping to step back
as far as you need, all the way to the start of the match.

MATCH HISTORY
Every finished match is saved right on your watch — winner, final score,
duration, breaks, and serve percentage for both players. Your last 50
matches are always one tap away.

SINGLES, DONE RIGHT
Built specifically for singles play, best of 3 or best of 5 — no clutter,
no modes you'll never use.

No account. No ads. No subscription. No internet connection required —
everything stays on your watch.
```
