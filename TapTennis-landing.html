Match formats

Built for real matches, not just rallies

Set the rules once and TapTennis plays by them — change anything and it starts a clean new match automatically.

FORMAT Best of 3 or Best of 5 Play a quick set or a full match — TapTennis tracks sets won and switches ends on schedule.
SERVE Choose first server Set who serves first before the coin toss even lands.
SPEED No-Ad scoring Sudden-point deuce for faster games when you're short on time.
DECIDER 10-point match tiebreak Skip a full deciding set with a 10-point breaker instead.
SAFETY NET Undo, one point at a time Mis-tapped a point? Step backward through your score history instantly.
NEW GAME Fresh match, one tap Start over any time — your settings carry forward, and the match you just played is saved to your history.
HISTORY Your last 10 matches Every finished match is kept on-device with its result, set scores, and date — review or delete them in Settings.
