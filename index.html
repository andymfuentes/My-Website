TapTennis

A tennis scoring app for Apple Watch that makes keeping score during a match simple and effortless — one tap per point, and it handles games, sets, tiebreaks, and deuce automatically. A companion iPhone app mirrors the live score in real time.

Swift· SwiftUI· watchOS· iOS· Watch Connectivity
