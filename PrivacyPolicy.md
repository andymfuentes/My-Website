## Summary

**CourtSwipe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**
Everything the app stores stays on your Apple Watch.

## What CourtSwipe stores, and where

CourtSwipe saves a local history of your finished tennis matches — winner,
final score, duration, and basic stats like break count and serve
percentage — directly on your Apple Watch, using Apple's standard on-device
storage (`UserDefaults`). This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is never transmitted over the internet — CourtSwipe makes no network
  connections of any kind
- Is not synced to iCloud or any other service
- Is not shared with the developer or any third party
- Is automatically limited to your most recent 50 matches
- Is deleted if you delete the app

## What CourtSwipe does NOT do

- No account or sign-in of any kind
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising or ad networks
- No third-party SDKs or dependencies of any kind
- No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health
  data, or any other sensitive device capability — the app never requests
  any of these permissions
- No cookies or web tracking (the app doesn't use a web view or make web
  requests)

## Children's privacy

Because CourtSwipe collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not
knowingly collect information from children, and there is nothing to
collect regardless of a user's age.

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional
iPhone sync), this document will be updated and the effective date above
will change accordingly. As of this version, the statement above is
complete and exhaustive — there is no hidden data collection beyond what's
