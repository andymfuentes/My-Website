TennisTap is a tap-to-score board for Apple Watch. This page covers how it works, answers to common questions, how to reach us, and our privacy policy.

How TennisTap works

TennisTap turns your Apple Watch into a full tennis scoreboard. Tap once each time you win a point, and TennisTap tracks games, sets, and match score for you — love, 15, 30, 40, deuce, and advantage — right on your wrist. No phone required, no account, nothing to set up.

  1. 01Open TennisTap and tap "New Match" to start at 0–0.
  2. 02Tap the screen once every time you win a point. TennisTap updates the score and calls games and sets automatically.
  3. 03Glance at your wrist between points — the current game, set, and match score are always on screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I undo a point I tapped by mistake?

Swipe left on the score screen and tap "Undo Last Point." TennisTap keeps a short history within the current game, so you can back out a mis-tap without losing the rest of the match.

My taps aren't registering mid-match — what's wrong?

The most common cause is Water Lock. If you turned it on to keep sweat or rain from triggering the screen, the display stops accepting taps until you turn it off — twist the Digital Crown to unlock and clear the water.

If Water Lock is off and taps still aren't landing, dry the screen and your fingertip, and check that your watch face isn't obstructed by a screen protector edge.

Does TennisTap handle tiebreaks and no-ad scoring?

Yes. TennisTap switches to tiebreak point-counting automatically at 6-6 in a set, and you can turn on no-ad (sudden death at deuce) scoring from Settings before a match starts.

Do I need my iPhone nearby, or an internet connection?

No. TennisTap runs entirely on the watch and works in Airplane Mode, out of Bluetooth range of your phone, or on a court with no signal at all.

Does TennisTap save my match history?

The final score of your last few matches stays in the app so you can check back after you're off the court. It's stored only on your watch — see the Privacy section below for details.

Which Apple Watch models does TennisTap support?

Any Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later. TennisTap does not require a paired iPhone to be present while you're playing.

Will keeping score with TennisTap drain my battery?

TennisTap is a simple, screen-on scoreboard, so battery use is comparable to any single app you keep open during a match — roughly what you'd expect from an hour or two of active use.

How do I start a new match without closing the app?

Force-press or swipe down on the score screen for the match menu, then choose "End Match." You'll be dropped back at "New Match," ready for 0–0.

Privacy policy

Data collected by TennisTap: love. Nothing about you or your matches ever leaves your Apple Watch.

Specifically:

TennisTap does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information on any server. Your match scores are kept only in the app's local storage on your own watch, so you can review recent results — deleting the app deletes that data with it. TennisTap does not use HealthKit, location services, third-party analytics, or advertising SDKs, and it does not require an internet connection to function.

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that uses data — this page will be updated first, and the "effective" date below will change accordingly.
