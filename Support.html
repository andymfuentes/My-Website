Help with TapTennis

TapTennis is a tap-to-score board for Apple Watch. This page covers how it works, answers to common questions, how to reach us, and our privacy policy.

How TapTennis works

TapTennis turns your Apple Watch into a full tennis scoreboard. Tap once each time you win a point, and TapTennis tracks games, sets, and match score for you — love, 15, 30, 40, deuce, and advantage — right on your wrist. No account, nothing to set up, and no phone required to play.

  1. 01Open TapTennis — it starts a fresh match at 0–0, ready to play.
  2. 02Tap the screen once every time you win a point. TapTennis updates the score and calls games and sets automatically.
  3. 03Glance at your wrist between points — the current game, set, and match score are always on screen.

Prefer a bigger screen? TapTennis includes a companion iPhone app that mirrors the live score in real time — set your phone on a bench or net post so everyone can follow along. The watch stays in charge of scoring; the phone is a read-only second screen, and turning it sideways shows a giant, court-readable score.

Frequently asked questions

How do I undo a point I tapped by mistake?

Tap the Undo button — the back-arrow at the bottom of the scoreboard. Each tap steps back one point at a time through the match, so you can back out a mis-tap without losing the rest of the score. It's greyed out when there's nothing left to undo.

My taps aren't registering mid-match — what's wrong?

The most common cause is Water Lock. If you turned it on to keep sweat or rain from triggering the screen, the display stops accepting taps until you turn it off — twist the Digital Crown to unlock and clear the water.

If Water Lock is off and taps still aren't landing, dry the screen and your fingertip, and check that your watch face isn't obstructed by a screen protector edge.

Does TapTennis handle tiebreaks and no-ad scoring?

Yes. TapTennis switches to tiebreak point-counting automatically at 6-6 in a set, and you can turn on no-ad (sudden death at deuce) scoring from Settings before a match starts.

Do I need my iPhone nearby, or an internet connection?

No. Scoring runs entirely on the watch and works in Airplane Mode, out of Bluetooth range of your phone, or on a court with no signal at all.

The optional iPhone companion app is just a live mirror — if you want to use it, keep your phone paired and nearby so the two can stay in sync. It's never required to keep score.

Can I see the score on my iPhone?

Yes. Open TapTennis on your iPhone while a match is running on your watch, and the current score appears and updates live. It's a read-only mirror — you still tap to score on the watch. Hold the phone upright for stacked rows, or turn it sideways for a giant score you can read across the court.

A small row of icons at the bottom of the phone shows the connection status; when they're all green, the phone and watch are fully in sync. If the score looks stuck, make sure the watch app is open and the two devices are close together.

Does TapTennis save my match history?

No — TapTennis tracks only your current match. It isn't saved between matches: tapping Reset or starting a New Match clears the board, and nothing is written to permanent storage. If you'd like to keep a result, jot it down before you start again. See the Privacy section below for details.

Which Apple Watch models does TapTennis support?

Any Apple Watch running watchOS 26.5 or later. TapTennis does not require a paired iPhone to be present while you're playing; the optional companion mirror app needs an iPhone on iOS 26.5 or later.

Will keeping score with TapTennis drain my battery?

TapTennis is a simple, screen-on scoreboard, so battery use is comparable to any single app you keep open during a match — roughly what you'd expect from an hour or two of active use.

How do I start a new match without closing the app?

Tap the red Reset button — the circular-arrow at the bottom of the scoreboard — to start fresh at 0–0 with your current settings. When a match finishes, a result screen also pops up with a New Match button that does the same thing.

Privacy policy

Data collected by TapTennis: love. Nothing about you or your matches ever leaves your own devices.

TapTennis does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information on any server. Your match score lives only in the app while you're playing — it isn't saved to permanent storage and is cleared when you reset or start a new match. When you use the optional iPhone companion, the live score is sent straight from your watch to your own paired iPhone over Apple's on-device Watch Connectivity link — it never touches a server or the internet. TapTennis does not use HealthKit, location services, third-party analytics, or advertising SDKs, and it does not require an internet connection to function.

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that uses data — this page will be updated first, and the "effective" date below will change accordingly.
