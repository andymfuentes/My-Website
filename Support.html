What we collect

TennisTapWatch does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information on any server. There are no accounts to create and no sign-in.

The only data TennisTapWatch keeps is your match scoring itself — the current game, set, and match score, and the final scores of your recent matches so you can review them after you're off the court. This data:

  • ✓is stored only in the app's local storage on your own Apple Watch;
  • ✓never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • ✓is not linked to your identity, an account, or any advertising profile;
  • ✓is deleted the moment you delete the app from your watch.
30–0

What we don't touch

TennisTapWatch does not use HealthKit or workout data, location services, the microphone, the camera, your contacts, or your Apple ID. It contains no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting or tracking libraries.

TennisTapWatch does not require an internet connection to function and makes no network requests of its own. You can use it in Airplane Mode, out of range of your iPhone, or on a court with no signal at all.

40–0

Children's privacy

Because TennisTapWatch collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The app is suitable for players of all ages.
